Sixplex zoning Has allowing six homes on a lot added homes? An estimate from building permits

An experiment, not a verdict. This page estimates what one zoning change has done so far, from public building permit records. It counts applications to build, not finished homes, and the estimate is uncertain.

The short answer

What changed: a natural experiment

Since May 2023, a house lot anywhere in Toronto can hold a building of up to four homes without special permission. In June 2025 City Council raised that to six, but only in the eight wards of the old city of Toronto and East York. City staff had recommended six everywhere. Council left the other wards at four, free to opt in later.

That split makes a comparison possible. Both parts of the city share a housing market, interest rates and a permit office, and differ in one rule.
