Sixplex zoning Has allowing six homes on a lot added homes? An estimate from building permits

An experiment, not a verdict. This page tries to put a number on a zoning change using public permit records. It counts applications, not finished homes, over a short period, and its own checks show how fragile the number is. It is not an evaluation of the policy.

One city, two rules

Since May 2023 a house lot anywhere in Toronto can hold a building of up to four homes without a rezoning. On 25–26 June 2025 City Council raised that to six, but only in the eight wards of the old city of Toronto and East York. Staff had recommended six everywhere. Council left the other wards at four, free to opt in later. The City's multiplex page still lists six homes only for those eight wards and one other, below.

That split is useful. The two parts of the city share a housing market, interest rates, a building code and a permit office, and differ in one rule. If the eight wards pulled ahead of the rest after June 2025, the rule is a likely reason. That comparison is what this page makes: how many homes builders proposed in each group of wards, before the change and after it.
